Loopo Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026. Loopo is a mobile game published by Raddi (Sriram Goparaju). Contact: sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game gives you an anonymous player ID so your progress can be backed up and your Daily score can appear on the leaderboard.
  • We use Google services (Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob) and RevenueCat to run the game, understand how it is played, fix crashes, show ads and handle purchases. They receive the data described below.
  • We do not sell your data.

What the game collects

  • Anonymous player ID (Firebase Authentication). Created automatically on first launch, not linked to your Google account.
  • Game data (Cloud Firestore): your progress (best scores, levels and stars, coins), settings, the time you last played, and your display name. This is your cloud backup.
  • Display name and Daily scores: the Daily Loop leaderboard shows your display name (a random name such as "Quick Otter 12", which you can change) and your score to every player. Do not use your real name if you don't want it shown.
  • Usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase): what happens in the game (runs, levels, ads watched, purchases made), app version, device model and operating system, country and language, and an app instance ID.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what went wrong, the state of the app, device details and an installation ID.
  • Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config): country, language, time zone and app/OS version, used to send game-balance settings.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can estimate a general location), device and diagnostic information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalised only where you have agreed; otherwise they are not.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat): what you bought and when, linked to an anonymous ID, so purchases can be restored. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
  • On your phone only: your settings and progress are also stored on the device, and reminder notifications (hearts full, new Daily Loop) are scheduled on the device. No message is sent from a server.

Why

To run the game and keep your progress, to show the Daily leaderboard, to understand and improve the game, to fix crashes, to show ads that pay for the free game, to process and restore purchases, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

Who receives it

Google (Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob) and RevenueCat, as service providers. AdMob may share data with its advertising partners to show and measure ads, as described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. All data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for consent before personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications are off until you allow them, and can be turned off in the game's Settings.
  • To delete your cloud backup and leaderboard entries, email us with your display name and the date you last played; we will delete them within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone.

How long we keep it

Game data is kept while you play and deleted on request. Daily leaderboard entries are kept so past days can be shown. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for its standard retention periods (Google Analytics: up to 14 months).

Children

Loopo is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
