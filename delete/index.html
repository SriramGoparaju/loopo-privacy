Delete your Loopo data

For the Loopo game on Google Play, published by Raddi (Sriram Goparaju). Loopo has no accounts or logins, so deletion is done by email.

How to ask for your data to be deleted

  1. Open Loopo, tap Settings, and note your display name (for example "Quick Otter 12").
  2. Email sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com with the subject "Loopo: delete my data". Include your display name and roughly when you last played.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  4. Uninstall Loopo from your phone. Otherwise the game makes a new cloud backup the next time you play.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your cloud backup: progress, best scores, levels and stars, coins, settings and display name.
  • Your entries on the Daily Loop leaderboards.
  • Your anonymous player ID.
  • Everything stored on your phone, when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of in-app purchases for refunds, tax and accounting, for as long as the law requires. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports. These are not linked to your display name. They are deleted automatically: Google Analytics data within 14 months, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) within 90 days.
  • Advertising data. This is held by Google AdMob under Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

More detail: Loopo Privacy Policy.
